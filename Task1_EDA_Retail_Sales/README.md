## Task 1 – EDA on Retail Sales Data

### Objective

Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to
identify sales patterns, customer behaviour trends and actionable
business insights.

### Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

### Analysis Performed

- Dataset structure and data quality inspection
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Quarterly sales trend analysis
- Customer age-group analysis
- Gender distribution analysis
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation analysis using heatmap
- Additional business-focused visualization
- Business insights and recommendations

### Key Deliverables

- Clean and structured EDA notebook
- Time-series visualizations
- Customer demographic analysis
- Product and category analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Actionable business recommendations

### Project Files

- `KhushiChaudhary_Task1.ipynb` – Complete EDA notebook
- `retail_sales_dataset.csv` – Dataset used for analysis
- `screenshots/` – Important analysis outputs and visualizations

### Outcome

The analysis provides insights into sales trends, customer demographics,
product performance and relationships between numerical variables,
supporting data-driven retail business decisions.
